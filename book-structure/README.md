## Notes
- Source is a four-layer study text (Sanskrit, word-split, Hindi, English) of 698 half-verse lines; page counts are estimated from all four layers, so they overstate the root text.
- Source: digitized study text of the 1888 Marathi-printed edition; Hindi and English layers are modern aids, not the original. Questions rest on the Sanskrit root text first and avoid the lines listed in the source's "Uncertain lines" appendix.
- The quiz tests what the text states ("the text says"), never advice on treatment or practice.
- Not covered: recitation, meter, correct practice under a teacher, clinical skill.
