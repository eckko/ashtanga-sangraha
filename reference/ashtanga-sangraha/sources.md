## Notes
- Conversion: none needed (already Devanagari). Mechanical changes only: NFC, collapse runs of spaces and trim, drop blank lines, remove trailing verse/chapter numbers like `॥ १ ॥` (logged), Part 3 sandhi join (ISSUES.md). The raw uploads are not copied into `raw/` (about 6 MB); sha256 above identifies them. `raw/structure-md-user-upload.md` is kept because it is small.
- Chapter boundaries: each chapter starts at its `अथातो … व्याख्यास्यामः` opening line (plus any non-blank lines directly above it, such as invocations or the verses before 6.11) and runs to the line before the next chapter. Part 1 chapter 1 also holds the title and invocation lines at the top of the file. `tools/build_text.py` does this.
- Not used on purpose: `reference/txt/*-hindi.txt` and `*-eng.txt` already in the repo (the repo README says these are AI-written, and the skill forbids adding self-written translations), and the older `reference/txt/ch01.txt` (differs from the uploaded Part 1, see ISSUES.md). No Hindi or English files exist for this book in `text/`.
